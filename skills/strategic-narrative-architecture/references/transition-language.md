# Transition Language — Connective Tissue Between Acts

The sentences that bridge Acts without feeling forced. Use these as calibration examples — the actual transitions must be written for each specific deck. These are models of the *move*, not scripts to copy.

---

## Act I → Act II (World → Crack)
*Moving from "here is the landscape" to "here is what's broken in it"*

- *"And yet — something isn't working."*
- *"The brands in this space know all of this. Which is exactly the problem."*
- *"Everyone sees the opportunity. Almost no one sees what's standing in front of it."*
- *"This is the world [Brand] operates in. Here's what it hasn't noticed."*
- *"The market is growing. The audience is changing. And the communication is staying exactly the same."*

---

## Act II → Act III (Crack → Turn)
*Moving from "here is the tension" to "here is the reframe"*

- *"Unless you look at it differently."*
- *"But what if the problem isn't what we think it is?"*
- *"Here's the thing: this isn't a [category] problem. It's a [human truth] problem."*
- *"The insight that changes everything is this:"*
- *"Turn it around, and the gap becomes the strategy."*

---

## Act III → Act IV (Turn → Answer)
*Moving from "the reframe" to "the strategy"*

- *"Which means there's one thing [Brand] can do that no one else can."*
- *"So the strategy becomes simple — not easy, but simple."*
- *"This is what we'd build from that."*
- *"One idea. Everything flows from it."*
- *"The answer isn't complicated. It just requires the courage to be the only brand saying it."*

---

## Act IV → Act V (Answer → Vision)
*Moving from "the strategy" to "the world it creates"*

- *"In three years, if this works, here's what changes."*
- *"This is what [Brand] becomes."*
- *"Not a campaign. A position. And here's what it looks like from the outside."*
- *"The work builds toward one thing:"*
- *"The goal isn't awareness. It's ownership."*

---

## Recovery Transitions
*For when the narrative has stalled mid-deck and needs to be pulled back on track*

- *"Let's bring this back to the person at the center of it."*
- *"The data says one thing. The human truth says something more interesting."*
- *"Before the executions — the idea that makes all of them possible."*
- *"All of this only works if we start with one belief:"*

---

## Notes

**Length**: Transition language should be short. One sentence. Two maximum. If you need three sentences to bridge two acts, the acts are not adjacent — there's a missing beat.

**Rhythm**: Transitions should feel like a pause and a pivot, not a summary. Don't recap what just happened. Bridge to what's next.

**Voice**: Transitions are the strategist's voice speaking directly — not the brand's voice, not a neutral narrator. They carry point of view.
