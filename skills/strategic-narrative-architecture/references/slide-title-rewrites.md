# Slide Title Rewrites — Labels Into Arguments

Before/after rewrites across common strategy deck sections. The principle: every slide title should be a claim, not a category. Read in sequence, the titles should tell the whole story.

---

## Situation / Context Slides

| ❌ Label | ✓ Argument |
|---|---|
| Market Overview | The market changed. The brands in it didn't. |
| Category Context | This category has been saying the same thing for ten years. |
| Competitive Landscape | Everyone is fighting for the same position. Which means it's available. |
| Market Size | A 4-billion market. And no one owns the emotional high ground. |
| Current Situation | [Brand] has awareness. It doesn't yet have meaning. |
| Background | Here's what we know. Here's what everyone knows. |

## The Title Sequence Test

Read only the slide titles, in order, aloud. Ask:
- Does it tell a complete story?
- Could someone who missed the deck understand the argument from titles alone?
- Does each title move the argument forward, or does it just label what's on the slide?

If the titles read like a table of contents, rewrite them as a sequence of claims. The titles are the skeleton of your argument — they should be able to stand without the flesh.
